## Featured projects

### FitNextFy — Fitness & Nutrition Platform

A fitness platform focused on converting daily tracking data into useful feedback for training and nutrition decisions.

* Daily check-ins and progress tracking.
* Dashboard and analytics for adherence and evolution.
* Training/nutrition planning and follow-up flows.
* Authentication and persistent user data with Supabase.
* Built with **Next.js, React, TypeScript, Supabase, Tailwind CSS and Recharts**.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-Open-22C55E?style=for-the-badge\&logo=vercel\&logoColor=white)](https://fitnextfy-1kt3.vercel.app/)
[![Source Code](https://img.shields.io/badge/Source_Code-GitHub-181717?style=for-the-badge\&logo=github)](https://github.com/anglfer/fitnextfy)

### Finance FEX — Personal Finance System

A personal finance application designed to make monthly money management easier and more structured.

* Income and expense organization.
* Recurring obligations and monthly planning.
* Spending and savings tracking.
* Financial overview focused on helping users understand where their money is going.

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/Live_Demo-Open-22C55E?style=for-the-badge\&logo=vercel\&logoColor=white)](https://finance-fex.vercel.app/)
